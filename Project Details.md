# Real Life Solution for Waste Management

## 1. Approved Project Title

**Real Life Solution for Waste Management**

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Waste management has become a major challenge in modern cities and rural areas. Improper disposal of waste causes environmental pollution, health problems, and poor sanitation. Traditional waste collection methods are often inefficient due to lack of proper monitoring and communication between citizens and waste management authorities.

Many citizens face difficulties in reporting waste-related issues, tracking waste collection requests, and receiving timely services. To overcome these challenges, a digital waste management system is required to streamline waste collection, monitoring, and reporting processes.

This project aims to provide an effective platform where users can submit waste collection requests, waste collectors can manage assigned tasks, and administrators can monitor the entire system efficiently.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

### Main Objective

To develop a smart waste management system that improves waste collection efficiency and promotes environmental cleanliness.

### Specific Objectives

* Provide an online platform for waste collection requests.
* Improve communication between citizens and waste management authorities.
* Reduce waste accumulation in public places.
* Encourage proper waste segregation and recycling.
* Track waste collection activities effectively.
* Generate reports for administrative monitoring.
* Increase public awareness about waste management.
* Promote a cleaner and healthier environment.
